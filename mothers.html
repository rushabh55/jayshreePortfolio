Nutrition

Nutrition guidance is centred on supporting healthy development during pregnancy, improving nourishment for both parent and baby, and strengthening recovery after delivery.

  • Nutrition support for mother and baby during pregnancy and after delivery
  • Designed to promote good health and stronger nourishment through each stage
  • Helps reduce the risk of vitamin and mineral deficiencies with practical dietary guidance
  • Can also include guidance on fenugreek and other natural galactagogues as part of nutritional support

Exercise

Exercise guidance is provided to support comfort, mobility, physical strength, and better recovery during and after pregnancy, with practical movement advice suited to each stage.

  • Supports strength, mobility, and comfort during pregnancy
  • Helps improve recovery and physical confidence after delivery
  • Can include safe movement practices suited to changing stages of pregnancy
  • Ask About Sessions

Lactation

Lactation and breast feeding guidance is available to support feeding confidence, comfort, and early postpartum care for parents who need practical help after delivery.

  • Emphasizes the importance of breast feeding and early feeding support
  • Addresses common lactation and breast feeding problems, especially in the early weeks after delivery
  • Offers continued support so new concerns and anxieties can still be discussed after sessions
  • Ask for Support

Labor

Labour preparation includes breathing, relaxation, and hypno birthing approaches that help parents approach birth with greater calm, confidence, and readiness.

  • Prepared for breathing, relaxation, and labour readiness support
  • Can include guidance for calmer labour and hypno birthing preparation
  • Supports confidence and emotional readiness before birth

Welcome Home Baby

This program guides expectant mothers in building a calm and meaningful connection with their baby during pregnancy. It introduces gentle practices that encourage intentional communication, emotional bonding, and a more conscious inner state while the baby is still in the womb.

  • Teaches mothers how to interact with and respond to their baby during pregnancy
  • Uses relaxed, alpha-wave states to encourage calm, focus, and positive emotional influence
  • Supports bonding in ways that may nurture the baby's growth, development, and overall wellbeing
  • Helps mothers create a peaceful internal environment through guided awareness and connection
